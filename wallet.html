How loans stay fair
  • Your wallet can never go below zero. Debt is tracked separately on your record.
  • When a loan is overdue it's collected automatically from your wallet and bank, but you always keep at least 500 MWD to play with, and your daily bonus is yours to keep.
  • Collection happens before you spend, so debt can't be dodged by moving money around, and you can't send away money you owe.
  • Whatever can't be collected stays owing and is collected as you earn. You can't borrow again while a loan is overdue.
  • Everyone can see your credit record (paid on time, paid late, overdue) so lenders know who they're lending to.
