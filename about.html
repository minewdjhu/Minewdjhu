What is Minewdjhu?

Minewdjhu is a made-up economy, built to be fun whether or not anyone else is online. Markets move on their own, you can launch your own creator-market, bank your winnings for interest, play the arcade, and watch it all narrated live in Discord.

Wallet vs. Bank

Your wallet is for trading, immediately usable. Your bank is for saving, slower to access, but it earns interest over time, and it's where Twitch channel-point rewards land directly.

Creator-markets & equity

Anyone can launch a creator-market. You start owning 20% of it as your own equity stake, completely separate from what investors put in. As the market's value grows, so does your equity's value. You can redeem part of it for real wallet MWD any time, capped per transaction so you can't drain it all at once.

Risky Creators & Risk Recovery

A creator only gets flagged "risky" after verified incidents, never just from bad ratings. If a verified incident causes a real crash, players who tagged that market early (and held the tag long enough that it isn't a last-second snipe) can share in a recovery payout. It's a fictional comfort mechanic, not real financial protection.

How the economy updates

There's no constant background loop grinding away on a small server. Markets update when someone actually looks, the backend calculates everything that should have happened since the last visit, in one step, the moment it's needed.

Discord

The Discord bot announces what happens, market moves, events, news, but never controls the economy. All control lives in the Creator Control Room. If the bot goes offline, nothing about the game itself is affected.

The obvious disclaimer

This is a game. MWD isn't real money, can't be exchanged for real money, and has no value outside this website.
